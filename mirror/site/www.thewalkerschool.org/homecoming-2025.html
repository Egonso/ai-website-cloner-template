HOMECOMING 2025

Get Ready for Walker Homecoming – October 24, 2025!

Get ready for Homecoming - Friday, Oct. 24!

Homecoming is a time for celebration at The Walker School for students, families, faculty, alumni and the community. The week leading up to the big homecoming football game will be filled with fun dress-up days and school spirit-building events across all divisions. Our biggest community event will be 2025 Fall Festival and Tailgate Party leading up to the Wolverine's Homecoming game. Check this page for all the details about this exciting week at The Walker School.

All of the festivities will take place on Walker's main campus (Fall Festival will be on Coles Field behind Coles Gym), and a variety of food options will be available for purcahse so families can remain on campus until game time. The Fall Festival is sponsored by The Walker School Association (WSA), with POTA selling pizza at the event. The Tailgate Party is sponsored by The Wolverine Club. Bring your tailgate chairs, friends and neighbors to enjoy a great afternoon at Walker. 

Block off the afternoon and spend the day at Walker! It's going to be a fun event for the whole family!

SCHEDULE

The Walker School Homecoming – Friday, Oct. 24, 2025

  • 2 p.m.  - Pep Rally and Upper School Walk-outs - Coles Gym (students only)
  • 3:15 p.m. - Fall Festival at Homecoming Begins/Football Concessions Open
  • 5:30 p.m. - Alumni gathering begins - Bowden courtyard (invitation only)
  • 6 p.m. - Tailgate begins - Tailgate Lot behind the press box
  • 6 p.m. - Fall Festival ends - Families move to Tailgate Party
  • 7 p.m. - Performance by the cheer squad
  • 7:30 p.m. - Wolverine Football Game
    • Halftime - Homecoming Court

Saturday, Oct. 25, 2025

  • 8:00 p.m. - Homecoming Dance for Upper School - on campus
fall festival

The Fall Festival at Homecoming is a free community event hosted by The Walker School Association (WSA) on the Friday afternoon before the football game. We have bounce houses, photo ops, arts & crafts, and carnival games for the entire family - including the return of the Faculty Dunk Tank!

Who: Primary School*, Lower School*, Middle School** and Upper School students and families; extended family, neighbors and friends are welcome.

Where: the foodball field behind Coles Gym; use Coles Courtyard to access the Festival. Look for the balloons!

When: Fall Festival begins at 3:15 p.m. and runs until 6 p.m.

Food: Snacks, food and soft drinks are available to purchase at the Fall Festival. Cash, card and Venmo accepted.

  • POTA Pizza
  • Decorative Funnel Cakes
  • The Wolverine Club will open football concessions at 3:15 p.m.
  • King of Pops will also be at the Fall Festival. They take cash, cards and Apple Pay. 

Parent Supervision and Parent Pavilion: Parent supervision is required for ALL children during Homecoming Events and the Fall Festival. Follow your division notes for specific dismissal instructions. The WSA Parent Pavilion is new this year and available at the Fall Festival for parents and adults to socialize during the event. 

Fall Festival Schedule:

  • 3:15 p.m. - Fall Festival begins
  • 3:30 p.m. - Photo opportunity and Autograph Session with Varsity Football Players
  • 3:30 p.m. - Face Painting begins
  • 3:45 p.m. - Faculty Dunk Tank begins
  • 6 p.m. - Families move to Tailgate Party
SPIRIT DAYS

The theme this year is "Fall into HoCo" and the Spirit Dress Up Days are listed below.

  • Monday 10/20: Team Spirit(s) Day – Wear a jersey from your favorite team (football, basketball, baseball, soccer, etc.)

  • Tuesday 10/21: Pumpkin Patch Day – Dress in cozy fall outfits like flannels, boots, and scarves; or as something from a pumpkin patch (scarecrow, pumpkin, corn stalk)

  • Wednesday 10/22: Wacky Wednesday – “Boo! I’m You!” Swap styles with your teachers for the day

  • Thursday 10/23: Creepin’ It Cozy – PJ Day; wear your comfiest or spookiest pajamas

  • Friday 10/24: Harvest Hues – Show class pride by dressing head-to-toe in your class color for Field Day and Pep Rally. Primary School will wear maroon and gold!
TAILGATE PARTY

Please join the Homecoming Tailgate Party at 6 p.m. hosted by The Wolverine Club in the lot immediately behind the press box. The party is FREE and open to all featuring games, activities, music and the opportunity to connect with Walker families in a fun, relaxed setting. Food can be purchased from concessions on site.

We encourage families to stay on campus between the Fall Festival and 7:30 p.m. football game kickoff, and the Tailgate Party will provide great entertainment!
VOLUNTEER OPPORTUNITIES
Whether you have an hour or the whole afternoon, WSA is seeking volunteers for carnival games, inflatables, arts & crafts, and supplies, too.  Fall Festival is a school-wide event, so Primary to Upper School parents are welcome. New this year: a Parent Pavilion to connect with other parents. CLICK THE LINK to learn more and sign up.
