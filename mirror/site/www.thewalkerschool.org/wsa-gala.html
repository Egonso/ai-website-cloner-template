WSA Gala: An Enchanted Garden

Saturday, Feb. 28, 2026

Ivy Hall, Roswell

Join us for the WSA Gala: An Enchanted Garden, to benefit The Walker School.

The Annual Auction starts Feb. 25, and will conclude at the WSA Gala on Feb. 28. The Gala is a community wide event and includes dinner, open bar, music and our fabulous silent auction items up for bid. Early Bird tickets are sold out, however there are limited quantities of general tickets. The past two years this event has sold out, so purchase your tickets today at wsa.givesmart.com. Early Bird ticket holders receive a sneak peek to the Annual Auction website before it goes live. Proceeds from the Gala and Auction will benefit the cause "Outfit the Outdoors", a campus enhancement plan to improve outdoor areas for our entire community.

There are many ways you can help ensure the event is a success:

  • Sponsor the event and receive special recognition, perks and advertising opportunities
  • Purchase tickets before they sell out!
  • Donate to the Annual Auction

Date: Saturday, Feb. 28, 2026
Time: 6 - 10 p.m.
Location: Ivy Hall - 85 Mill Street, Roswell, Ga.
Attire: Garden Party Attire / Outdoor Elegance
*This event is for adults only. Attendees must be 21 or older.

Contact us at: WSA.Auction@thewalkerschool.org

GALA EVENT DETAILS

Let’s celebrate a wonderful year at Walker and support the WSA Auction with an evening of music, dinner and fun! All proceeds benefit The Walker School and tickets include:

  • Dinner
  • Open Bar
  • Online auction
  • Music and dancing
  • Entertainment

Schedule of events:
6:00 p.m. - Cocktail Reception
7:00 p.m. - Dinner
9:30 p.m. - Auction Closes
10:00 p.m. - Event Concludes

Location:
Ivy Hall - 85 Mill Street, Roswell, Ga.
Directions to Ivy Hall

Tickets include:

  • Cocktail Reception with hors d'oeuvres, open bar
  • Curated dinner, open bar and a signature cocktail
  • Silent auction featuring community experiences and unique offerings
  • A special unveiling of WSA's "Outfit the Outdoors" campus enhancement plan

Attire: Garden Party Cocktail
Join us in the spirit of an Enchanted Garden: think relaxed cocktail attire with floral touches, soft colors and a bit of sparkle. Note: the evening will flow between indoor and outdoor spaces on two levels, so we recommend comfortable shoes or stylish flats to keep you moving with ease.

 For attire inspiration click here!

This event is for adults only. Attendees must be 21 or older.

ANNUAL AUCTION

The 2026 Annual Auction is open February 25-28. The Auction is The Walker School Association’s largest fundraiser.  The auction is online and all items will be available to take home from the Gala. In 2025, we raised more than $60,000 with the help of your generous donations!

Early bird ticket holders receive a sneak peek of the Annual Auction website and the chance to purchase "Instant Items" early, such as special student party experiences and break dress code tickets.

We encourage your participation at any level, whether it's donating items, active bidding, attending the Gala, or all three. Please know your support truly makes a difference in our community. Donations are due Jan. 30, 2026.

Contact us at: wsa.auction@thewalkerschool.org

Through your support, WSA and the Annual Auction have contributed to significant enhancements around campus:

2025

  • Tricycle track for Primary School
  • Gaga ball pit for Lower School
  • Modern, flexible furnishings in Bowden Library
  • 300 Halloway Renovation including new furnishings and carpet
  • Outdoor furniture for Middle School
  • Furniture for Leithead Boardroom

2024

  • Bowden Library was transformed into a collegiate-style student center with modern furniture where students can collaborate as groups or have quiet space. And currently, we are refreshing Winship Library with modern, flexible furnishings.

2023 

  • Faculty and Staff lounge transformed into a welcoming space with upgraded furniture, new appliances and modern decor

  • Production curtains and a full-length step installed in Gatti Hall stage

  • Primary School received a new Art and Science library, a sensory light table and classroom

2022

  • Outdoor furniture in Bowden Courtyard
