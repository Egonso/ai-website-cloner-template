ABOUT US

DIVERSITY AND BELONGING

“Belonging is something that Walker does very well. The willingness to see the value of each individual person and bring that out is why we are here. It’s a foundational element of developing a good human being.”

– Walker Parent
 
Shea Fleming

Director of Diversity and Belonging

Email

At Walker, we respect the infinite worth of everyone. We believe in the dignity of the individual and are committed to developing a cohesive, inclusive and diverse community where everyone – no matter their perspective, culture, background, religion or ethnicity – feels that they belong.

Our graduates are leaders who excel in a diverse and ever-changing world, and that vision begins with encouraging students, faculty, staff and parents to develop a perspective that embraces diversity and enhances global awareness.

The Walker School provides a welcoming, caring community dedicated to academic excellence and strong extra-curriculars that encourage our students to express themselves and prepare them to lead. Belonging is important to us, and we strive to create a school environment where all students feel there is a place for them.

At Walker, we actively seek opportunities for interaction and meaningful dialogue to nurture a sense of belonging, eliminate disparities and foster equality.
