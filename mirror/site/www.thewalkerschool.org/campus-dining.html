COMMUNITY

CAMPUS DINING

Flik Independent School Dining has been Walker’s food service provider since 2023.
 

Flik Menu
 

Flik is one of the largest independent school dining providers in the country and serves over 285 schools, including 18 schools in Georgia. Its mission is to empower a community of kindness through great food, with respect for each other and our amazing planet.

Flik focuses on well-prepared and fresh food. They have local culinary professionals and dieticians who work strategically with schools to cultivate menus and manage allergies. Flik enhances our dining experience in each of the divisions including the after-hours cafe.
